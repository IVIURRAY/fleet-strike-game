# Map Design

The map is rendered a bit like google maps with differnet zoom levels.

The goal of the game is to conqure the known galaxy

Routes between planets (trade lanes) have been estabilished over time in these galaxies, the fleet also uses them to move around

So there is basically a linear path through the glaxy that both sets of units follow along in the first iteration of the game

Along the trade route there are planets. those planets have moons.

In order to capture a planet, your fleet must destroy the enemy presents and raise your own flag on the plannet.

Moons are great at hosting defensive structures like turrets or sheilds.

Planets can produce manpower, ships and resources based on which building you place on your planets

Your main planet should be called your captial planet and like in civ it has a special building that give you the base resources of the game
